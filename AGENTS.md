# Project rules

- Biolystes pages (src/) must import their own images from `src/assets`, never from `lystes/src/assets`: the `lystes/` folder is overwritten when it is resynced from the other project, so files can disappear and break the build.
- Keep BioPage's SalonSlides on Embla with a shared data-driven slide template, so each slide can have distinct central and side media without duplicating its layout.
- Keep additional-product eligibility in `src/data/offres.ts` and test it independently of presentation, so pack descriptions cannot drift from the rule.
- Keep the embedded creators gallery's sector row off biolystes.com's home page through the `sansOnglets` flag on `HomeCreatorsGallery` (set in the cosmetique entry of `BlocsAccueilSecteur`), so sector names coming from the embedded view cannot appear on the home page; re-apply it after `lystes/` is resynced.
- Keep /agents-ia's creators gallery in « équipes IA » mode via the `equipes` flag on `HomeCreatorsGallery` (AgentsVisionRawPage), so it shows lystes.ai's AI teams rather than plain creators; re-apply it after `lystes/` is resynced.
- Keep the Biolystes Agents IA team-card adaptations in its local vision template (cosmetic photography only, no try-on card), so the imported general-purpose Lystes content cannot reintroduce unrelated examples; re-apply after resync.
- Account links (signup/login) always resolve (also the hardcoded inscription links in lystes landing Header/Footer and BioPage) through `src/lib/comptes.ts` to biolystes.pro, including the `adresseCompte` hook in `lystes/src/lib/lien-lystes.tsx` and the connexion link in the agents-vision template; re-apply after `lystes/` is resynced, so no account button can point to lystes.ai.
- Catalogue availability badges and orderability go through `src/data/disponibilite.ts`, never by editing the synced `src/data/catalogue-pro/products.ts`, so a resync cannot reintroduce orderable « à venir » products.
- Every « Commencer mon projet » entry (home hero project field) goes through `src/lib/demarrer.ts` to biolystes.pro/demarrer, also when empty; re-apply in lystes BioPage/HomeHeroVideo after resync.
- Keep the `export` on `HomeExperienceCarousel` in `lystes/src/pages/BioPage.tsx` so the /a-propos page mounts the same photo carousel as the home page; re-apply it after `lystes/` is resynced.
- Keep the « Gérer mes cookies » link (`to: "#cookies"`) in the Légal column of `colonnesBiolystes` in `lystes/src/components/landing/Footer.tsx`; `src/components/BandeauCookies.tsx` listens for clicks on `a[href="#cookies"]` to reopen the consent banner. Re-apply it after `lystes/` is resynced, so visitors can always change their cookie choice.
- Google Analytics (G-B5TSLW9P7Y) lives only in `index.html`, once, after a `gtag('consent','default')` that denies everything; the visitor's choice is stored by `src/lib/consentement.ts` (same key read in `index.html`). Never add a second GA/GTM tag, and never grant consent by default.
